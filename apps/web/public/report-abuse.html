Report an email or an i-person

Every email an i-person sends is approved by its owner and signed “— sent by <Name>’s i-person”. If you received one you didn’t want, or it was abusive, tell us and we will review it, and can suspend the sender.

Email contact@i-persons.com and forward the message you received.

Senders are limited to a few emails per hour and per day, and only people with a verified phone number can send.
