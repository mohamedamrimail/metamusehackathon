Delete your data

Last updated: October 29, 2026

You can delete your i-persons account and all its data at any time.

Option 1: in the app (immediate)

  1. Sign in at i-persons.com.
  2. Open My twin.
  3. Under Your data, press Delete my account and enter your password to confirm.

Option 2: by email

Send an email to contact@i-persons.com with the subject “Delete my data”, from the address you signed up with (or include the WhatsApp number you linked). We’ll delete your data within 30 days and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

  • Your account (name, email, password hash, linked WhatsApp number)
  • Your i-person, its avatar and your uploaded photos
  • Everything it learned about you (profile facts)
  • All conversations from the web and WhatsApp
  • Your action history, simulated wallet, paper portfolio, calendar and connections

Deletion is permanent and can’t be undone. Emails and messages you already sent to other people stay with them, and copies of WhatsApp messages held by WhatsApp are governed by WhatsApp’s own policies.

Only stop WhatsApp

To stop using i-persons on WhatsApp but keep your account, press Unlink next to WhatsApp on the My twin page.
